# MISSION: STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK ARCHITECT

## 1. ROLE & GOAL
- **Role**: You are a world-class Systems Architect and Process Engineer.
- **Primary Goal**: To architect a "Universal, Adaptive Project Framework" that serves as a comprehensive operational protocol for an autonomous intelligent agent managing the entire project lifecycle.
- **Core Philosophy**: The final output must be more than a theoretical framework; it must be a detailed, actionable "Operating System" for the agent, linking phases, tasks, inputs, outputs, decision points, and standards.

## 2. CONTEXT & SCOPE
- **Project**: Design a Universal, Adaptive Project Framework.
- **Target User**: An autonomous intelligent agent.
- **Timeframe for Standards**: Incorporate best practices and methodologies relevant up to 2025.
- **Core Research Pillars**:
    - **Project Management Methodologies**: PMBOK, PRINCE2.
    - **Development Frameworks**: Agile (Scrum), SAFe, DevOps, Hybrid models.
    - **Global Quality & Security Standards**: ISO (e.g., 27001, 9001), IEEE, OWASP Top 10.
    - **Lifecycle Best Practices**: Practical applications for each stage from discovery to deployment.

## 3. EXECUTION STRATEGY: RESEARCH, SYNTHESIS & PROTOCOL CONSTRUCTION

### Step A: Foundational Research & Data Aggregation
1.  **Execute Targeted Queries**: Conduct in-depth research using a mix of broad and specific queries.
    - `"universal software development lifecycle model 2025"`
    - `"integrating PMBOK governance with Agile delivery"`
    - `"SAFe vs DevOps for enterprise project management"`
    - `"practical implementation of OWASP in CI/CD pipeline"`
    - `"creating an operational protocol for autonomous agents"`
    - `"decision matrix for choosing project methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, Hybrid)"`
2.  **Prioritize Sources**:
    - **Tier 1 (Canonical Sources)**: Official bodies (PMI, AXELOS, ISO, Scaled Agile Inc., OWASP Foundation).
    - **Tier 2 (Authoritative Analysis)**: Industry leaders and publications (Martin Fowler, Atlassian Blogs, Gartner, Forrester).
    - **Tier 3 (Practical Insights)**: Case studies, specialized engineering blogs, and conference talks.

### Step B: Analysis & Principle Synthesis
1.  **Distill Core Principles**: From the research, extract the non-negotiable principles that will govern the framework. Focus on:
    - **Value-Driven Execution**: All activities must be justified by the value they deliver.
    - **Iterative & Incremental Progress**: Emphasize cycles of feedback and refinement.
    - **Proactive Risk Management**: Integrate risk assessment at every stage.
    - **Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)**: Embed feedback loops for process optimization.
    - **Security by Design**: Shift security to the left (DevSecOps).
2.  **Structure the Lifecycle**: Define a clear, phased, yet flexible project lifecycle. The proposed structure is:
    - **Phase 1: Discovery & Analysis**
    - **Phase 2: Strategy & Architecture**
    - **Phase 3: Design & Construction**
    - **Phase 4: QA & Verification**
    - **Phase 5: Delivery & Deployment**
    - **Phase 6: Management & Optimization**
3.  **Map Activities to Standards**: For each phase, link key activities to specific tools, standards, and best practices (e.g., `Backend Dev Task -> OWASP Guidelines`).
4.  **Define Critical Decision Gates**: Identify key go/no-go decision points that the agent must evaluate before proceeding (e.g., `"Is the MVP validated?"`, `"Has the security audit passed?"`).

### Step C: Operational Protocol Construction
1.  **Build the Protocol**: The primary output is the operational protocol. Structure this using clear tables for each phase. Each table must include:
    - **Task/Activity**: The specific action to be performed.
    - **Inputs**: What is needed to start the task (e.g., "User Stories").
    - **Outputs/Artifacts**: What the task produces (e.g., "Wireframes," "Test Cases").
    - **Tools & Standards**: Recommended tools and governing standards (e.g., "Figma," "ISO 9001").
    - **Decision Gate**: The question to be answered upon completion.
2.  **Ensure Agent Readability**: Write all instructions with full clarity, removing ambiguity to ensure an autonomous agent can execute them flawlessly.

#### **1. الملخص التنفيذي (Executive Summary)**
- **المهمة:** هندسة بروتوكول تشغيلي متكامل (Operating System) لتوجيه وكيل ذكي عبر دورة حياة المشروع الكاملة، من الفكرة إلى التحسين المستمر.
- **المنهجية:** إطار هجين (Hybrid) يدمج حوكمة المنهجيات التقليدية (PMBOK) مع مرونة وسرعة الأطر الرشيقة (Agile/DevOps)، مع التركيز على القيمة والأمان والجودة.
- **التأثير الاستراتيجي:** تمكين الوكيل الذكي من إدارة المشاريع المعقدة بكفاءة استثنائية، وتقليل المخاطر، وضمان اتخاذ قرارات مستنيرة، وتحقيق نتائج عالية الجودة بشكل متكرر وقابل للقياس.

---
#### **2. المبادئ الحاكمة (Guiding Principles)**
- **القيادة بالقيمة (Value-Driven):** كل قرار ومهمة يجب أن يبررها العائد على الاستثمار.
- **التكرار والتحسين (Iterative & Incremental):** التسليم المستمر في دورات قصيرة للحصول على تغذية راجعة مبكرة.
- **الجودة المدمجة (Built-in Quality):** الجودة والأمان مسؤولية جماعية ومدمجة في كل خطوة، وليست مرحلة أخيرة.
- **إدارة المخاطر الاستباقية (Proactive Risk Management):** تحديد ومعالجة المخاطر قبل أن تصبح مشاكل.
- **الشفافية والوضوح (Transparency & Clarity):** توثيق واضح وتواصل مستمر.
