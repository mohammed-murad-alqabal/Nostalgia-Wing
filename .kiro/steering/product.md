# Wing of Nostalgia Product Overview

**Wing of Nostalgia** (جناح الحنين) is a sophisticated, emotionally intelligent Flutter application designed to deepen relational bonds through shared memories, gratitude, and spiritual connection.

## Core Purpose
Unlike typical social or journaling apps, Wing of Nostalgia leverages a custom **Psychological Engine** to analyze emotional states and provide tailored recommendations that foster connection and well-being.

## Key Features
- **Psychological & Emotional Analysis**: Advanced emotional gravity engine, surprise & evolution engine, and emotional state tracking
- **Memory Management**: Create, view, and share cherished memories with intelligent "Time Capsule" features
- **Gratitude Journal**: Dedicated space for recording daily blessings integrated with psychological engine
- **Spiritual Connection**: Quranic verses and spiritual reflections curated for emotional resonance
- **Immersive UI**: Enhanced parallax effects and glassmorphism design with smooth animations

## Target Audience
Users seeking deeper emotional connections, spiritual growth, and meaningful memory preservation in their relationships.

## Technical Philosophy
The application follows **Clean Architecture** principles with a unique focus on emotional intelligence and psychological adaptation systems that dynamically adjust the user experience based on emotional state analysis.
